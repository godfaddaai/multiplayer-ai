# Acceptable use policy

Effective August 2, 2026.

mpai is for collaboration among people who are authorized to access the
machines, tailnet, provider accounts, code, and AI sessions involved.

Do not use mpai to:

- access, observe, prompt, or retain another person's session without their
  informed authorization;
- monitor employees, contractors, students, or other people covertly;
- bypass Tailscale, provider, operating-system, workplace, or account security
  controls, including approvals, permissions, CAPTCHA, checkpoints, or 2FA;
- expose an mpai host, invitation, credential, transcript, or support artifact
  to the public internet or another untrusted party;
- obtain or disclose source code, credentials, personal data, regulated data,
  or confidential material you are not authorized to handle;
- introduce malware, destructive prompts, denial-of-service traffic, or other
  activity intended to compromise a device, account, service, or person;
- impersonate another participant or remove the human-attribution marker from
  a remote prompt;
- use the alpha for safety-critical, high-impact, regulated, or adversarial
  workloads for which it has not been certified;
- violate applicable law or a third party's terms or rights.

Hosts are responsible for choosing teammates, roles, and shared sessions.
Participants are responsible for prompts they send and for respecting the
host's authorization. Use selected-session sharing and viewer roles when broad
access is unnecessary. Revoke access immediately when a teammate no longer
needs it.

The maintainers may decline support, close community access, or coordinate a
security response when project infrastructure is used in a way that threatens
users or the project. Because the software is self-hosted and MIT-licensed,
the maintainers do not remotely control an installation; local owners must
enforce their own organizational and legal requirements.

Report a suspected vulnerability through GitHub's
[private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/godfaddaai/multiplayer-ai/security/advisories/new),
without including live tokens or private transcripts.
